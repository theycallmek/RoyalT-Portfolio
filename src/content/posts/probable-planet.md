excerpt: "Hugh Ross presents himself as a bridge between faith and science. As an astronomer with a PhD from the University of Toronto and founder of the apologetics ministry Reasons to Believe, Ross occupies a unique position in the origins debate. He is neither a young-earth creationist who dismisses modern cosmology nor a secular scientist who rejects the divine. His book Improbable Planet: How Earth Became Humanity's Home promises readers the best of both worlds: a scientifically literate defense of creation that embraces the age of the universe and the findings of modern astronomy."
